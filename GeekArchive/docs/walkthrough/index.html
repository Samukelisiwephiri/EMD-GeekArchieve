Step-by-step guide · 23 steps

Geek Archive walkthrough

One full journey through the app: a student shows a project with a demo video, a spaza-shop owner finds it, sends an offer with a stipend, the student accepts, and the shop signs off the finished work. Every screenshot is from the real app.

Before you start

  • Run the app with npm run dev in the GeekArchive folder and open http://localhost:3000.
  • Demo mode (no Supabase keys): any password works and nothing is saved.
  • Stay in one browser tab and don't reload. Demo data lives in the open page; a reload starts over.
  • For the video, any MP4, WebM or MOV up to 50 MB.

Part 1

The student shows their work

Thandi signs up, gets verified with her university email, and posts a project with a demo video.

  1. 1 Open the site

    The landing page explains Geek Archive and holds the Sign In / Sign Up card. Scroll down for “What makes Geek Archive different” and “How it works”.

  2. 2 Sign up as a student Student
    1. Click Sign Up, then Youth Pod Talent.
    2. Fill in full name, mobile number, “I am a” (Student), university, email and the password twice.
    3. Tick the privacy notice and click Create Account.

    Every field is required. If something is missing or wrong, the reason shows right above the button.

  3. 3 Get verified Student
    1. Click Verify now in the banner.
    2. The university is already filled in from sign-up. Click Verify with my university email.

    A .ac.za email verifies instantly. Other emails go to an admin for review.

  4. 4 Save a talent profile Student
    1. Open My profile.
    2. Choose the pod role (Developer), add availability, skills and a portfolio link, then Save my profile.

    The profile is how a student gets matched into pods. SMMEs can view it; contact details stay private.

  5. 5 Post a project with a demo video Student
    1. Open My projects and fill in the name, GitHub link, category and description.
    2. Under Demo video (required), click Upload demo video and choose the file.
    3. Check the preview, then Post my project.

    You can Replace or Remove the video before posting, or paste a YouTube, Vimeo or Loom link instead.

  6. 6 The project is live Student

    The card shows Shown to organisations, with Watch demo and GitHub repo buttons. Use the switch to hide it, or Edit and Delete.

    Projects without a demo video stay hidden from organisations and show an “Add demo video” prompt.

    Now click Sign Out (top right).

Part 2

The SMME finds the student

A spaza-shop owner signs up, an admin approves the business, and the shop discovers Thandi's project.

  1. 7 Sign up as an SMME SMME
    1. Click Sign Up, then SMME / NGO.
    2. Fill in the contact person's name, mobile number, organisation type, registered name and CIPC number (e.g. 2019/123456/07), then email and password.
    3. Tick the privacy notice and Create Account.

    NGOs choose NGO / NPO and give their NPO number. The organisation's name is what students see.

  2. 8 Ask to be verified SMME
    1. Click Verify now. The name and CIPC number are already filled in.
    2. Click Send for review, close the window and Sign Out.

    Until an admin approves, the SMME can't see student projects or contact students.

  3. 9 Approve the business Admin
    1. Sign in as admin@geekarchive.demo (choose SMME / NGO) and agree to the privacy notice.
    2. Under Verification requests, check the CIPC number and click Approve. Then sign out.

    Rejecting asks for a reason, which the user sees.

  4. 10 Discover projects SMME
    1. Sign in again as joe@tuckshop.co.za.
    2. Open Discover projects.

    Search as you type, filter by skill or category, or describe a challenge to Let AI find the best project.

  5. 11 Watch the demo SMME

    Click the video preview on the card. The project window opens and the video plays straight away, with the description, tech stack and GitHub link below.

  6. 12 View the student's profile SMME

    Click View Thandi's profile to see her role, availability, skills and all her projects. Her phone number and email are never shown.

  7. 13 Send a message SMME
    1. Close the window and click Contact Thandi Mokoena on the card.
    2. Edit the pre-filled message and click Send message.

    The card then shows “Message sent · waiting for the student to reply”.

Part 3

Working together

The shop sends an offer, Thandi accepts it, and the shop signs off the delivered work.

  1. 14 Send an offer SMME
    1. Open Describe a need, describe the problem and click Find a project & assemble a pod.
    2. Geek Archive suggests Thandi's project and a 3-role pod.
    3. Fill in What you're offering and a short message, then Send offer.
  2. 15 Wait for an answer SMME

    Pod workspace shows the offer and each student as “Offer sent · waiting for an answer”. Approve sign-off stays greyed out until a student accepts. Sign out.

  3. 16 Read the message Student
    1. Sign in as thandi@uj.ac.za and open Messages.
    2. Click I'm interested (or Not now).

    “I'm interested” shows a Reply by email button, and the SMME sees her answer.

  4. 17 Read the offer Student

    Open Received offers. The offer shows who it's from, what they offer, their message, their need and Thandi's task, with Accept and Decline.

  5. 18 Accept it Student

    Click Accept: “You accepted. You're working with Bra Joe's Tuckshop.”

    Decline removes the offer, and the SMME sees “Declined”.

  6. 19 See the pod Student

    My pods now shows the project, who it's for and Thandi's task. The header counts it as an active pod. Sign out.

  7. 20 See the answer SMME

    Sign in as the shop and open Pod workspace. Thandi shows as Accepted, and the stage reads “Students accepted: work in progress”.

  8. 21 Sign off the work SMME

    When the work is delivered, click Approve sign-off. The pod moves to “Delivered & Signed Off” at 100%.

  9. 22 Privacy & my data Anyone

    The lock icon (top right) opens the privacy notice, a summary of your data, Download my data and Delete my account, as POPIA requires.

  10. 23 On a phone Anyone

    Everything works at phone width: the tabs move into a menu, and the project video fills the screen.

Not covered here: the AI project analysis and AI recommendations need an Anthropic API key, and real accounts, saved data and video storage need Supabase keys plus the database script (supabase/schema.sql).
